## 👤 About Me:


Hello, I’m **Daniel Mukisa** — a learning mentor, support partner.  
I’m passionate about **data science, mentoring learners, and community development**, and I thrive on simplifying complex concepts into clear, visual explanations that empower others.

### 🎯 What I Do
- Mentor learners in **Python, pandas, NumPy, and scikit‑learn**    

### 🌱 My Interests
- Professional development and career growth   
- Creating accessible technical resources for workshops and mentees  
- Benchmarking machine learning models with visualizations and comparisons  


### 💡 My Approach
I believe in **clarity, accessibility, and collaboration**.  
Whether it’s guiding a mentee through their first dataset, refining workflows for efficiency, or sharing motivational reflections, my goal is to **equip others to succeed**.
